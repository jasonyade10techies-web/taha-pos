## Review focus

- Historic refunds must never appear as incoming cash collections.
- No due date means no invented midnight; settled debt must not remain overdue.
- Quick product creation inside receiving must create catalogue only and preserve parent draft.
- New mobile sales may not bypass Windows offline promises; reject unsafe states on the server under the same store transaction lock.
- Missing historical private media must remain a visible recovery limitation, not an authorization bypass.
